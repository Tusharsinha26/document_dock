03.2
Chronometry

Proactive expiry radar. Not reactive panic.

A deadline is only useful if you know about it with sufficient time to act. Passports take 4 to 8 weeks to renew; vehicle checks require booking windows. DocumentDock factors lead times into every calculation.

  • Color-coded status indicators: Valid, Expiring Soon, Expired, and Lifetime.
  • Customizable notice periods (e.g. 60 days for visas, 30 days for MOT).
  • Chronological upcoming timeline preview right on your dashboard.
